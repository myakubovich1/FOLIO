# Experimental scorecard v1
Version folio-v1.0.0. Synthetic software demonstration only; no outcome validation, probability of default, lender adoption or fairness certification.

Each source covers explicit complete months. End-exclusive coverage is used; ordinary profiles need ≥90 days and ≥3 months; seasonal profiles need ≥730 days and ≥24 months. Incomplete coverage, unknown classifications, absent balance/obligation evidence, mixed currencies or invalid records cause abstention. No score for absent history; no traditional credit input.

Income I is sum of positive income records. Expenses E = absolute sum of negative expense records minus positive refunds, floored at zero. Transfers and borrowing are excluded. Amounts and sums use safe integer minor units, one currency per assessment. Demo obligations are documented included expenses; never add them twice. Average monthly expenses = E / complete-month count. Rounding happens only at display/final score; retained factor values reproduce the result.

Three fixed factors (same required inputs for every scored borrower): capacity = 50 × clamp((I−E)/I ÷ 0.4, 0, 1); liquidity = 30 × clamp(closing liquid balance / average monthly expenses / 3, 0, 1); cycle resilience = 20 × clamp(minimum documented month-end liquid balance / average monthly expenses, 0, 1). Index = round(sum(contributions)), range 0–100. Balances come from separate synthetic balance evidence, not inferred from partial transactions. Corrections change flow metrics but do not fabricate revised source balances. Zero income/expenses requires clarification. Seasonal aggregation uses the full two-year cycle; no income-timing or occupation penalty.

Evidence confidence is a separate coverage assessment (complete synthetic source with confirmed classifications, or limited/unresolved). It is not calibrated or a risk score. Every factor references source record IDs; passport omits raw records and identifiers. Coverage, source label, model version, uncertainty, revision and validation limitations are mandatory in every snapshot. Currency-unit scaling preserves ratios; identity and country labels are not score inputs. No cross-market ranking.

Validation also rejects impossible calendar dates, records outside end-exclusive coverage, unsafe or inconsistent balance values, and categories contradicting transaction direction (income/refund/borrowing must be inflows; expenses must be outflows). Corrections preserve the source category. Monthly summaries are computed on the server from the same interpreted rows. Charts show income and gross spending with a common maximum over monthly totals; an accessible table lists exact amounts and refunds. Period expenses are net of refunds. Monthly source summaries are excluded from shared passports, which disclose period totals only.
