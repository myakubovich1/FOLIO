# Devpost submission material — draft, not submitted

**Title:** FOLIO — credit beyond your credit history

**Tagline:** Turn permissioned financial evidence into an understandable experimental assessment and a portable, controlled financial passport.

**Problem and audience:** A missing traditional credit file need not mean missing financial evidence. Folio demonstrates an alternative pathway for a US newcomer, freelancer, Kenyan seasonal farmer and merchant, while making limited evidence explicit. It does not simulate a bureau rejection or claim lender acceptance.

**What we built:** A borrower can consent to sources, review and correct interpretations, inspect a deterministic alternative scorecard or justified abstention, ask evidence-grounded questions, preview disclosure, export a PDF and create or revoke an expiring recipient link. Evidence confidence is separate from financial capacity; missing data never becomes a favorable assumption.

**Nessie contribution:** Actual server-side requests to a dedicated synthetic banking customer, resource capability checks, normalization, source references and database persistence. The resulting records are used by the deterministic assessment and issued sandbox passport. Optional feed failures and absent historical balances remain visible. Insert the actual live smoke-check result here after credentials are configured; do not claim successful live ingestion before that check passes.

**Gemini contribution:** Structured PDF extraction with source locations, broad financial classification and income-pattern suggestions requiring confirmation, and question-focused explanations selected from the owner's authorized evidence bundle. Financial calculations remain deterministic. Every displayed amount and factor contribution comes from named validated metrics, with unsupported claim fields/citations rejected. Insert actual model, prompt version, live generation/extraction results and separate quality measurements after running the configured evaluation.

**Technical depth:** Next.js/TypeScript, Auth0 owner identity, PostgreSQL/Timescale persistence and real continuous-aggregate cash-flow endpoint; deterministic scorecard and evidence-confidence contracts; exact-decimal ingestion, stable IDs, reconciliation, immutable snapshots, hashed shares and deletion guards. The grounded AI pipeline has human-authored evaluation cases for missing data, unsupported claims, misleading descriptions and numerical consistency. No predictive credit-risk accuracy or general fairness certification is claimed.

**Design:** Wise-inspired clarity with original Folio SVG identity, self-hosted typography, generous responsive layouts, mobile review cards, explicit provider states, inspectable financial explanations and a report that retains uncertainty. Wise assets/affiliation are not used.
