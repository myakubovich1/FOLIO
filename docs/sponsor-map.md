The published Nessie API lists account deposits/withdrawals/bills/loans and single purchase/transfer lookups; account purchase/transfer listing is capability-probed and may be unavailable. Account history never proves complete statement coverage or historical liquid balances, so sandbox-only ingestion correctly abstains.

Lockfile is the reproducible version authority: Next16.4.2, @google/genai2.30.0, express-openid-connect3.4.0 and pg8.23.1 were used. Model name is explicitly configurable and unselected until accessible credentials exist. Public resources/merchant descriptions are untrusted source content; they cannot supply prompt instructions or protected-trait scoring features.
